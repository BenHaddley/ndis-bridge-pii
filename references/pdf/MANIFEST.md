## Notes and limitations

- The URL for the Bullseye-to-Bookworm whitepaper that previously appeared in this wiki (`pip.raspberrypi.com/.../RP-006519-WP/...`) now returns **404**. The working location is the `pip-assets.raspberrypi.com` URL above. Raspberry Pi reorganises this portal, so re-check the link rather than assuming it is stable.
- `MS-RNDIS.pdf` is the 2014 release from Microsoft's download server. Its own dating is internally inconsistent (release stamp 1 May 2014 in the page header, revision 5.0 dated 15 May 2014 in the revision summary), and it has not been confirmed as the newest revision of the specification. Check Microsoft's Open Specifications library before treating it as current.
- Text could be machine-extracted from `MS-RNDIS.pdf` and `raspberry-pi-4-datasheet.pdf` only. The two Raspberry Pi product briefs, the Bookworm whitepaper, all three L3Harris sell sheets and both SitaWare flyers did **not** yield extractable text with the tooling available here, so nothing in the wiki cites them at section or page level. Read those directly before quoting them.
- **The five vendor documents (L3Harris, Systematic) are public marketing material.** They establish what the products are. They do not specify host interfaces, throughput, or integration mechanisms. Statements in the wiki about SitaWare's supported standards (CoT, RTSP, RTMP, STANAG 4609, NMEA 0183, GPSD, APIs/SDK) come from Systematic's public *open architecture web page*, not from these PDFs, and must be verified against your own licensed deployment.
- **Not held here, and needed:** the AN/PRC-171 / RF-9820S Interface Control Document and programming guide, and SitaWare integration/API documentation. Both come through programme and vendor channels. L3Harris tactical radio documentation is frequently export-controlled — do not commit restricted documents into this repository.
- `usb-cdc-ecm-120.pdf` is relevant if the 9820 turns out to present a CDC-ECM interface (Linux `cdc_ether`) rather than RNDIS. It ships inside a USB-IF archive that also contains an adopters agreement; check those terms before redistributing the file outside this project.
- Raspberry Pi documents are published under a Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives licence. Keep the originals unmodified and separate from bench notes.
- The Pi 4 and Pi 5 product briefs are marketing-level summaries. The Pi 4 *datasheet* (Release 1.1) is the one with the section-level USB and Ethernet detail (§5.3 USB onward).
